# Optical-only development response protocol, frozen before new interpretation

24 October 2026. Applies to the unchanged 24 date-supports and all 96 frozen source slots. This extends the existing REFERENCE_UNCERTAINTY/PROTOCOL.md to new evidence; previous responses are not reused or modified. No prediction/reference join, water map, independent reliability claim or accuracy inference is authorized by this packet.

## Display and information separation

Every available chip is offered, not just the clearest view. Use neutral aliases R01-R24 and observation numbers. Expose optical sensor, acquisition UTC, target acquisition interval, nominal native resolution, Landsat tier and source geolocation metadata where present. Do not expose discovery context/source tags, first-reader responses, SAR values, candidate scores or predictions, scene water percentages, SCL water/vegetation/bare classes or Landsat's water bit. A private administrator key preserves exact product lineage separately.

Primary RGB and NIR-red-green displays use fixed reflectance 0 to 0.30, gamma 1, across all sources. A fixed 0 to 0.08 dark-surface RGB sensitivity may be consulted but never replace the primary display or establish class. Clipping affects display only; retain negative and above-one source values numerically. Nearest-neighbour enlargement never adds physical resolution. Invalid source support is gray, not black water. The 490 m view and a fixed central 110 m view provide context; no adaptive cropping or percentile stretch. Axes are metres, north up, EPSG32750. Draw 10/30/50 m supports and the outer 90 m extent covering the 50 m support under the fixed +/-20 m displacement scenarios.

QA flags are diagnostic, never automatic observable class. S2 SCL1,3,8,9,10,11 indicate defective/saturated, shadow, cloud/cirrus or snow conditions. SCL0/missing QA is unavailable support. Do not screen out SCL2 just for darkness. Landsat QA_PIXEL bits0-5 and the selected RGB/NIR saturation bits plus terrain-occlusion flag are diagnostic. Do not use water bit7. An unflagged pixel is not proved clear; cloud, haze and shadow can be missed. Visible image evidence may contradict QA. Record those disagreements.

## Response dimensions

1. For each optical observation independently, annotate only defensibly visible W=open surface water, N=visible non-water surface, O=visibly obscured and U=unresolved. Visible canopy is a surface observation and does not rule out subcanopy inundation. Shadow, dark roofs, exposed surfaces and murky water can be ambiguous. Use U when alternatives cannot be resolved; never infer W from low NIR alone. Missing evidence yields U, not N. Qualify water even when persistent; history does not determine current class.
2. The primary support is the fixed 30 m square. Retain 10/50 m and all 25 displacements using x,y in {-20,-10,0,10,20} m. Save joint W/N/O/U vectors and their marginal envelopes; components sum to one for every scenario. These are interpretation-conditioned portions, not exact subpixel composition or statistical confidence bounds. Landsat's resampled 10 m cells remain replicated 30 m information. Do not invent 10 m boundaries from the display. Unknown absolute registration remains explicit; source scene RMS is not a certified local bound. Scenario envelopes do not cover all possible error.
3. Record optical-time interpretation, support sensitivity/registration, temporal evidence and target-acquisition eligibility separately. A resolved water-portion interval and possible-water interval including O/U are distinct. No new 0.5/0.8/0.9 semantic cutoff or numeric confidence threshold is adopted. Preserve visible MIXED and possible unresolved mixture.
4. Temporal E0=no interpretable/compatible evidence; E1=one-sided interpretation without a justified continuity bridge; E2=distinct bracketing observations with compatible resolved states and recognizable physical continuity, under explicit continuity/position assumptions; E3=additional sufficiently local independent intervening-event/physical support. No event evidence was acquired by the chip batch. Never fabricate E3. Absence of a reported event alone is insufficient. Different sensors/resolutions may disagree; preserve that disagreement rather than voting.
5. E2 can support only a conditional target-time interval formed by a hull, not an intersection, of compatible before/after intervals. Also retain the unconstrained transient-allowed bound [0,1]. This is not proof that the intermediate state falls within the endpoint hull. Record TRANSFERABLE only with its stated evidence strength and assumptions; use NOT_TRANSFERABLE for positive contradictory/inapplicable evidence, UNKNOWN when evidence is insufficient. E1 without a bridge remains target-time UNKNOWN. Seven-day search limits and small hour offsets are not transfer permissions.

An assistant first pass is not a genuinely independent second reader. Do not claim interpreter reliability from a repeat view, another model's response, or human endorsement of that model. Keep all original independent responses if a real second reader later participates. This development panel is purposive and clustered; conditional yield cannot be generalized as population accuracy. No old nine-conflict response is changed.

## Audit and cost

Original source-grid rounding/halo checks remain in acquisition receipts. Preserve all absent and failed slots. Stop if another firewall problem, fundamental evidence inadequacy or consequential semantic choice is encountered.
