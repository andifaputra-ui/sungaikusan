# Proposed next decision: reference-led development qualification, not another frozen-site rescue

24 October 2026. Proposal only. No locations moved, dates substituted, new imagery ordered or candidate predictions generated by this document.

## Recommendation

Allow a small **new development branch** that qualifies locations and dates from independently observable optical evidence before looking at SAR predictions. Preserve the failed 12-location/24-support panel intact. A static river midpoint, polygon representative point or coastline coordinate is a geographic discovery aid, not evidence that a 30 m support is an observable water interior or a difficult land surface. Do not repeat that selection mechanism hoping more dates will fix it.

The first reversible task would use the already acquired evidence to identify whether any support-matched interiors exist within those existing chips. Keep the same 30 m primary, 10/50 m sensitivities and positional scenarios. Record all nominated regions and reasons, including none found; no hidden replacements. Any exact new centre must be frozen before candidate scores are read. Visible state and clarity may deliberately inform this **development** discovery; it is independent of SAR performance, but it is not outcome-independent probability validation and must never be presented as such.

For example, the clear May shoreline views contain water away from the frozen central transition. That is a concrete existing-evidence opportunity to assess, not an already-qualified new site. Moving east would change the physical support and might enter coastal/ocean-masked or off-target water. Before accepting such a unit, verify the thesis-domain boundary, real optical support across all positional scenarios, source validity, optical-time state and temporal continuity. Retain OPERA ocean masks as unavailable/policy support, never automatically as a water error. Do not use coastal controls as proof of inland flood performance.

Keep the initial new branch at **up to four physical systems and eight date-supports**, using existing chips first, no new reference acquisition or Planet orders initially. At least observable inland water and a physically defensible difficult land case are necessary to claim the branch addresses the research bottleneck; ordinary vegetation plus coastal water alone can check gross functionality but cannot select a thesis detector. These are scientific coverage requirements, not sample-size sufficiency or numeric accuracy cutoffs. If missing contexts cannot be qualified from saved evidence, stop with that specific gap rather than process candidate SAR or silently nominate convenient controls.

A subsequent acquisition proposal, if needed, should nominate a concrete independently known inland system and an actual dated reference opportunity with asset-level access, quality/support, timing and quota/cost provenance. Planet may be a reference provider only when its real availability and purpose are justified; access does not guarantee a clear acquisition or a timing bridge. No generic archive rescue, paid order, new sensor-processing chain or expansion of the original ceiling starts from this proposal.

## Why this is a methodological boundary

This deliberately changes development discovery from source-tag-driven frozen centres to reference-observability-led locations/dates. The exposure is permanent development-only. The old failure/nonresponse ledger is not overwritten or improved by replacement. All old reader returns and conditional intervals remain immutable. A separately sealed probability sample with known inclusion probabilities is still necessary for final population inference, including cloudy/difficult conditions and the full thesis period. Selection from clear reference evidence is inappropriate as a shortcut to that final accuracy sample.

Do not compare models until actual reference support is informative for both observable water and land, difficult contexts are either represented or explicitly excluded from the intended narrow claim, exact S1/OPERA source/support pairing passes, and fixed publisher-derived operating points plus the comparison protocol are frozen. No local tuning or candidate-driven reference rewriting. If the resulting evidence can only falsify a gross error in a few controls, report exactly that and retain ambiguous candidate ranking.

## Alternatives and strongest objection

Running the original24supports anyway would mainly compare outputs under missing truth and could create a false winner. More source-tag sites or a looser automatic hour window would repeat the demonstrated problem. Treating optical QA water bits, OPERA or historical occurrence as truth would change source roles and generate circular evaluation. None is recommended.

The strongest objection to the proposed amendment is that choosing visible, well-supported optical cases can select easy surfaces and fair-weather conditions, suppress transient flood states, and encourage repeated human rescue until a preferred model looks good. Even without seeing SAR predictions, this can be an unrepresentative development test and can overstate confidence. Coastal interiors may be especially easy and may not match the inland thesis target or OPERA's mask policy. The protection is not a claim that such selection is unbiased: it is permanent development status, an explicit limited falsification purpose, complete nomination/failure records, a bounded workload, and a later genuinely separate probability audit. If those limitations are unacceptable, the defensible alternative is to pause semantic candidate comparison until acquisition-time references for inland water and difficult land can actually be obtained.

No final classifier, water map, training, old VV A/B, final reference release, full-series production or method freeze is proposed here.
