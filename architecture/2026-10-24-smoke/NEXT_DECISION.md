## Strongest objection to this recommendation

This could replace transparent weak rules with an opaque model that reproduces teacher-label and domain biases, while OPERA introduces a different processing chain, taxonomy and ancillary masking. The reference bottleneck may persist, and a hand-picked panel cannot establish population accuracy. Annual context could later encode historical answers and suppress real transitions. Consequently a technical pass is insufficient for adoption; independent hard cases, matched current observations, explicit missing support and a sealed final probability audit remain indispensable.
