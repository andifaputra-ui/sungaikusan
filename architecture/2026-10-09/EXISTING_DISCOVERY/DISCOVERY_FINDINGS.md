## Consequence for the next step

The selection rule does find ordinary/managed context and observation-quality challenges. It does **not** demonstrate a balanced panel containing persistent water, a genuine inundation/recession pair, a verified artificial dark confuser or a resolved small-water boundary. It is not evidence that no such features exist elsewhere.

The 12 supports occur on only four target dates (seven on one September2018 date and three on one November2018 date); time offsets range from about −115.43 h to +76.81 h. Distinct supports are not automatically independent physical systems. The native20 m SCL is represented by correlated10 m postings; its QA ranking did not guarantee visual clarity. Most importantly, these target dates do not match the existing eight processed SAR dates.

Use this result to avoid declaring the saved pool sufficient. Preserve the leads and failures; do not automatically search alternate renderings or re-adjudicate the old nine conflicts. The next proposed bounded public-optical pass should seek missing physical contexts and useful dated corroboration across the approved development scope, with independent interpretation. It remains a resource authorization boundary under the approved architecture, not permission to run A/B or new SAR.
