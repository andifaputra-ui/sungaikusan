# Development evidence programme after architecture approval

9 October 2026. Implements the approved two-programme architecture and the development-discovery amendment. This is a concrete preparation/acquisition specification, not a new probability sample or a released detector. The original architecture and every historical response stay immutable.

## Purpose and scope

Find independently interpretable counterexamples and ordinary controls before committing to a detector evaluation. The programme is deliberately purposive. Clarity, independent geography, physical diversity, timing and cost may guide development selection. Detector-informed or A/B-disagreement-based discovery is also permitted by the owner's amendment, with permanent development-only status. None of this panel estimates population accuracy, prevalence or final reference yield.

The accepted target is current-acquisition observable open surface water, including persistent and newly formed water. Canopy, flooded vegetation, contamination and historical water occurrence are not substitute target states. The current sigma0/C1 processing chain stays unchanged. Final inference remains a later, separately authorized probability audit of a locked candidate over the enumerated L1/S1A population.

## Workload and discovery routes

Retain the approved planning cap of 24 distinct physical systems and up to 48 date-support records. No minimum count certifies adequacy. Work through six overlapping challenge questions:

1. Persistent open water: does a current detector retain water without a change signal?
2. Inundation/recession: does it admit genuinely new water and remove receded water?
3. Smooth land: can independently identified artificial or natural surfaces falsify darkness-as-water?
4. Small/narrow/mixed supports: how do boundaries and registration limit identifiability?
5. Difficult observation: does missing support or poor geometry remain explicit rather than becoming land?
6. Ordinary controls: what does an enriched challenge panel miss about common land and broad-water settings?

Maintain a `discovery_route` for each proposal: independent geography, independent optical context, pre-existing engineering sensitivity, detector output, or A/B disagreement. Record exact source/version/hash, target timestamp, proposed physical system, footprint, selection rationale, failed attempts and decision. No arbitrary balance or equal quotas are promised. Physical-system independence must be defensible from geography; unresolved parent relationships are clustered/flagged, not counted as independent replications.

The first evidence route is already acquired development optical imagery and existing dated records. Previous engineering components can nominate difficult contexts (for example omission-sensitive or unsupported areas), but they cannot establish water, land or MIXED. The existing 56-unit component prototype contains no semantic A/B predictions. A disagreement claim requires actual authorized versioned A/B outputs; none exist in this continuation.

Allow an adversarial route when useful; do not require one before references exist. Selection and interpretation are different tasks. The discovery ledger holds detector information, while a neutral reference packet omits the selecting output, route, threshold, expected class, SAR image and prior reader answers. If a reader has already seen these, disclose the exposure; the reading may still be development evidence but is not blind. AI review must not be called an independent human replicate.

## Reference-source hierarchy and prospective acquisition envelope

Architecture approval authorized preparation, not new imagery acquisition (approved architecture sections 4 and 10). The next proposed authorization is one bounded public-optical development pass:

- Reuse saved evidence first. Do not reacquire identical assets merely to improve appearance or repeatedly rescue the old nine conflicts.
- For a nominated development system/date, inspect existing metadata for dated Sentinel-2 and Landsat coverage, actual support geometry, band/QA assets and temporal opportunities. Rank independently of detector success by defensible state timing, spatial resolution/support, source-contract clarity, then cost; preserve the ranked alternatives and the chosen rationale. Development selection may seek clear evidence, but the final probability sample will retain cloudy/nonresponsive units.
- Use the tested legacy S2 route only as explicitly marked display evidence. Quantitative optical indices remain blocked until exact-asset scale/offset provenance is verified. A metadata-consistent C1 route is a prospective alternative, not a silently adopted complete archive or replacement for legacy assets. Landsat requires mission-specific QA_PIXEL and QA_RADSAT treatment, source-domain nodata and per-asset calibration evidence. No 10 m detail may be fabricated from native 20/30 m information.
- Acquire at most four optical observation slots per proposed date-support: an informative near-time observation and, where material, before/after corroboration or a source alternative. Adjacent tiles and processing versions of one overpass count as one observation opportunity. Do not assume four timestamps are independent truths.
- Proposed incremental transfer ceiling: **0.75 GiB** for imagery payload plus **2 GiB** local output, up to 48 supports/192 slots; hard-stop before a request that could exceed the remaining cumulative allowance. Historical public-route extrapolation is approximately 0.63 GiB for that cap, not a guarantee. Count failed and partial transfers conservatively; metadata/headers and imagery are separately reported. Never reset a cumulative meter on retry.
- Planet, Airbus and other quota/paid orders remain **zero**. If public optical evidence cannot resolve narrow/artificial/dynamic cases, retain the failure and request a specifically costed VHR role/asset decision. Do not consume quota to improve convenient failures.
- Every pixel call requires source role, acquisition authority, complete native-window rounding/resampling halo/padding scope and lineage checks before file/network pixel access. No source read may be justified by a safe output crop alone.

The 0.75 GiB/2 GiB proposal is a smaller development-only tranche within the earlier 6 GiB/16 GiB planning scenario. Neither scenario is an active download budget yet. Source metadata work and saved-chip qualification used no new research imagery. A/B and new SAR are not part of this proposed acquisition tranche.

## Response and locking order

1. Register and permanently mark a nominated system/date as development. Freeze its discovery provenance before reference interpretation; additions are versioned, with reasons, not silently substituted.
2. Fix the disjoint 30 m target support on the accepted grid and retain 10 m centre and 50 m context sensitivities. Bound registration uncertainty independently of SAR agreement. Do not count representations, source bands or adjacent chips as repeated independent units.
3. Prepare neutral optical/QA/timestamp/support packets. Retain per-source display calibration status and actual resolution. Exclude prior answers, detector images, predicted class, selection rank and sampling strata. Hash the packet and blank response protocol before review.
4. Record optical-time W/N/O/U portions and interval limits, spatial/registration uncertainty, temporal continuity evidence, and final acquisition-time interval separately. Record before/after disagreement and possible intervening changes; no universal ±24/72h rule or proof from absent event reports. Conditional transfer assumptions and a no-transfer sensitivity remain explicit.
5. Preserve each reader's first response unchanged. For any later interpretation-reliability claim, require independently completed human readings with documented custody; agreement with an AI is not human–human reliability. A development reader may know the area, but exposure to the selecting prediction must be disclosed.
6. Freeze a reference-qualified panel before any separately authorized A/B comparison. One discovering output cannot be treated as an independent final test, even if the reference interpretation was blind. The final test requires a fresh locked-map probability design and new blinded responses; old development judgments are never copied into it.

## Adequacy and consequential boundaries

Do not use a universal two-water/two-land or count threshold. Ask whether independently supported open-water and non-water bounds can actually falsify each candidate, whether difficult contexts and repeated states are present, and whether temporal/support uncertainty is narrower than the decision being attempted. Publish missing challenge families and avoid extending a claim beyond them. Report conditional and assumption-relaxed results separately.

After the development evidence pass, recommend either a narrowly specified candidate comparison with an explicit selected-date full-source SAR/resource plan, or a specific reference-source/claim redesign. No automatic new SAR, A/B, semantic tolerance, L-band detector, reference-to-training role change, final release or method freeze follows. A negative result is retained, not repaired by selecting only cases where the proposed detector looks plausible.

The final 36-date/up-to-288-tile scenario remains unfunded and undrawn. An evidence-qualified development panel does not establish that final reference bounds or sample size will be adequate.
